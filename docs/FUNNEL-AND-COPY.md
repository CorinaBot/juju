Juju is the photobooth that walks the room — a branded, attended roaming robot that covers the floor you paid for, without a line.

Category: **corporate / brand-activation first. Wedding is proof of polish, not the headline buyer.**

## 2. What was wrong with the current funnel

- Juju lives as card #2 in a 7-product grid on a Miami production site.
- The live banner is a stacked contact sheet: clipart robot, orange checks, phone + email + URL + QR + five states. That is the “vibecoded” look.
- Public instinct to price by the hour ($395/hr) puts you in the gap where brides think you’re expensive and brand managers think you’re cheap.
- No org-type qualifier. Product-first forms attract the wrong lead.
- No data / CRM sentence. That is the single biggest unexploited gap versus Bosco, Snapbar, and Captured Celebrations.

## 7. Visual system

Color
- Ink black `#0B0B0C`
- Paper `#F4EFE6`
- Champagne gold `#C4A574` — rules and eyebrows only
- Purple — robot underglow only. Never a brand wash. Never a gradient blob.

Type
- Display: Cormorant Garamond (or similar editorial serif)
- UI / body: Outfit or a clean geometric grotesque
- Never: Inter + purple gradient, script fonts, neon, Comic inflatable “JUJU”

Photography rules
- Robot is the hero. Faces are proof, not stock “fun.”
- Prefer the real unit (square LED frame, dome camera head) over stylized humanoid robots.
- One line of type. Then stop.
- Banner: black field, giant JUJU, three lines, URL. No QR pile, no orange checks, no phone stack.

## 8. Internal rate card (never publish hourly)

Public page: no numbers.
Proposal: block price indexed to guest count + brand depth.

| Tier | Scope | Quote band | Anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Activation — Standard | ≤300 guests, 3–4 hrs, branded overlay + wrap, attendant, digital + opt-in CSV | $2,400–$2,800 | Captured Celebrations small / TLC 3hr robot |
| Activation — Launch | 300–800, 5–6 hrs, microsite, report | $3,200–$4,200 | Mid-size brand activation band |
| Activation — Flagship | 800+ / trade show / dual unit, 8+ hrs, producer | $4,800–$7,000 | Large festival / multi-experience |
| Tour / multi-city / install | custom | Custom | Smilebooth “Tour” / Bosco |
| Additional hour | any tier | $450–$500 | TLC published overage |
| Data / analytics module | opt-in + CRM-ready export + report | +$400 | Captured Celebrations analytics |
| UGC rights / curation | | +$300–$600 | same |
| Instant branded prints | | $0.75–$1.50 / print | same |

Two rules:
1. Price the block, not the hour. Hourly is only the overage line.
2. Branding + data are the ladder, not add-ons you mention if they ask.

$395/hr is an internal cost basis only. A 4-hour public $395 rate ($1,580) sits under every attended corporate tier in this category.

## 10. Trust stack to add (in this order)

1. Service geography, named: Miami / Florida · Atlanta · Virginia · Tennessee · North Carolina · South Carolina · travel
2. Response promise: same-day / under 2 hours during business hours — pick one and keep it
3. Insurance figure, when you have the cert. “Fully insured” is invisible to procurement. $2M or $5M on the page is not.
4. Case metrics later: sessions, shares, estimated reach. Use “estimated.” A logo wall without numbers only proves you were hired.

Do not invent Meta/Hilton-style marks you do not have.
